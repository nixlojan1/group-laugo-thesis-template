‎Background of the Study

‎The rapid advancement of information and communication technology has significantly
‎transformed how organizations manage operations and deliver services. At the global level, the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 9 (Industry, innovation, and Infrastructure) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), emphasize the adoption of digital systems to improve efficiency, accessibility, and sustainability of public and private services (United Nations, 2015). These goals highlight the importance of leveraging technology to optimize resource utilization and enhance service delivery at national, regional, and local levels. At the national and local context, sports facilities play a vital role in promoting physical activity, community engagement, and overall well-being. However, many sports facilities, especially small- to medium-scale establishments, continue to rely on traditional manual processes such ‎walk-in reservations, paper-based scheduling, and spreadsheet recording. According to Schwarz, Hall, and Shibli (2015) , manual facility management practices often result in ‎scheduling conflicts, inaccurate records, and inefficient utilization of resources. These issues negatively affect both facility administrators and users.
‎
Several studies have explored the development of web-based reservation and management
systems to address these challenges. Smith and Johnson (2022) found that web-based
scheduling systems significantly improve operational efficiency and reduce administrative
workload in small organizations. Similarly, Ahmad et al. (2024) developed a Sports Facilities
‎reservation System (STFS) that enables users to reserve sports facilities online, demonstrating ‎improvements in accessibility and user satisfaction.  

‎Despite these advancements, existing systems reveal notable research gaps. Most current
s‎ports facility management software focuses on single-facility operations and basic reservation ‎functions. There is a lack of systems that integrate comprehensive dashboards, real-time analytics, equipment borrowing management, and flexible pricing mechanisms tailored for s‎ports complexes. This gap represents a functional and technological research gap, as ‎current solutions do not fully address the operational complexity and  decision-support needs of facility owners and administrators.

In this study, a Sports Facility Management System refers to a web-based software application designed to automate the reservation, scheduling, monitoring, and management of sports facilities and equipment. The roles of the system include providing customers with an accessible platform for reservations and payments, while enabling administrators to oversee operations, monitor transactions, and manage resources efficiently.

‎The purpose of the proposed system, SportEase: A Web-Based Sports Facility Management ‎system, is to develop an integrated platform with separate dashboards for customers and administrators. The system aims to streamline reservation processes, minimize human error, enhance transparency, and improve overall service quality. By aligning with established software development practices and leveraging web technologies, the proposed system seeks to ‎contribute to improved facility management and user satisfaction (Perez, 2021; Smith & Johnson, 2022).
